About the practice

Adv. Mrunmai Pimparkar is a Pune-based advocate and in-house legal counsel specialising in intellectual property, media & entertainment law, technology & AI law and corporate advisory.

Her work spans cross-border content licensing, commercial contract negotiation, technology agreements, trademark and copyright protection, and startup structuring — across entertainment, SaaS, travel tech, sustainable commerce and professional services.

Her technical exposure at COEP gives her a practical lens for advising technology and engineering ventures. She has recently launched her individual practice.
